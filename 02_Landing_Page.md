## FAQ (collapsible, 5 items)

### Q1 — Who reads this audit?

Only our founding partners. Reports are not reviewed by sales staff, support staff, or external contractors. Your data is retained for 24 months unless you request deletion.

### Q2 — Will I be contacted after I submit?

You receive one email with your audit report. If your audit shows a strong fit, we'll send a case study after 48 hours. After day 7, you'll receive a calendar link to book a discovery call if you'd like one. If you prefer no further contact at any point, reply "stop" to any email.

### Q3 — Do I need to know everything to complete this?

No. Several questions include a "don't know yet" option. The audit still generates useful findings. If a question would require you to pull a specific number you don't have on hand, approximate — the audit is directional, not forensic.

### Q4 — Does this cost anything?

No. The audit is free. Our paid engagements begin after a free discovery call — the audit helps you decide whether that call is worth your time.

### Q5 — What happens to my data?

Encrypted in transit, encrypted at rest, access limited to the two founding partners named above. We do not sell, share, or syndicate audit data. Full privacy terms linked in the footer.
